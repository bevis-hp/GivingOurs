
Hackathon: DevHack with theme to tackle UN Sustainable Development Goal 9.

Idea: A platform to connect high-skill volunteers with small organisations in developing countries.

Like platforms like Lend With Care and Kiva but giving labour instead of loans. Initially targeting large UK employers that have employee volunteering days that are unused to give volunteering labour to countries in the global south initially targetting businesses in Nigeria. 

Similar to GoVo, Neighbourly and OnHand but instead of targeting volunteers directly we are targetting UK companies to get their employees lost volunteering days.

- Employers in UK sign up their employees up to the platform to be volunteers.
- Employees are then listed on the platform with their expertises, place of work and available days that they can "Give Hours".
- Businesses in developing countries then can book the volunteers slots.
