140m volunteering hours go unused each year, according to the Royal Voluntary Service.

Why the hours go unused

  • Too few flexible, one-off opportunities
  • Employees don’t know where to find them
  • Opportunities rarely match their expertise

How it works

Like microlending, but with expertise instead of loans.

Inspired by Kiva and Lend With Care, GivingOurs lets companies give hours of skilled labour where it’s needed most.

  1. 01

    Employers sign up their teams

    UK companies register and invite employees who have volunteering days to give.

  2. 02

    Employees list their skills

    Each volunteer shares their expertise, workplace and the days they can “Give Hours”.

  3. 03

    Businesses book a slot

    Small businesses in Nigeria book the expertise they need for a focused, remote session.
